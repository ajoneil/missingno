# PGO training ROMs

Game-shaped workloads used only to train profile-guided optimization during
release builds (`scripts/pgo-build.sh`). They are not part of the emulator,
its test suites, or any distributed build artifact.

| ROM | Author | Source |
|-----|--------|--------|
| `cgb/demotronic.gbc` | Demotronic (final) — 1.000.000 boys aka Megaboys (Optiroc, Matsomoto, Mathman), LCP 2002 | https://www.pouet.net/prod.php?which=7175 |

These are unmodified demoscene productions, included in the spirit in which
the scene distributes them, with attribution. If you are an author and want
a work removed, open an issue — it will be removed promptly.
